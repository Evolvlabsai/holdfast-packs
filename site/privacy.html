Holdfast Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026. Holdfast is made by EvolvLabs, LLC.

The short version

Holdfast does not collect, sell or share your personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. What you put in the app stays on your device.

What stays on your device

Your plans, contacts, meeting points, inventory, notes, photos and any other data you enter are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. Some of this data is encrypted on the device. If you delete the app, this data is deleted too, unless you made a backup.

The built-in AI assistant runs fully on your device. Your questions are not sent anywhere.

What the app downloads

The app connects to the internet only to download:

  • the content catalog (the list of available packs),
  • the News & tips feed, and
  • the map, routing and content packs you choose.

These files come from GitHub (github.com and github.io), operated by GitHub, Inc. The app sends no personal data with these requests. Like any web server, GitHub can see your IP address and basic device information when a file is downloaded. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Permissions

  • Location: shows where you are on the offline map and plans routes. Your location is never sent off the device.
  • Camera: scans barcodes for your inventory. Images are not saved or sent.
  • Photos: lets you attach pictures to your own records, if you choose.
  • Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and nearby devices: lets you share plans and packs directly with a nearby phone, without the internet. Nothing is shared unless you start it.
  • Notifications: shows download progress and reminders you set.
  • Biometrics: lets you lock the app with your fingerprint or face. Biometric data is handled by your phone and never reaches the app.

Backups and sharing

You can export a backup file or share a plan. You choose where it goes, for example a folder, a cloud drive or another person. After that, the place you chose controls the file. Firearms records are left out of backups and shared plans unless you include them.

Tips

Tips are optional and unlock nothing. Payments are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We receive no card details. The store tells us only that a purchase was made, so it can be completed. See the privacy policy of Google or Apple for how they handle payments.

Children

Holdfast is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/Evolvlabsai/holdfast-packs/issues.
