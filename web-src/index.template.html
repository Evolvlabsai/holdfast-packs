Your emergency plan, even with no signal.

Holdfast keeps offline maps, directions, first-aid cards and your family's plan on your phone. Set it up once at home. After that, it works with no internet and no cell service.

Coming soon Free on Google Play and the App Store. Maps cover the United States.

Everything stays on your phone

Pick your home state and the states where your family lives. Holdfast downloads them over Wi-Fi. When the towers go down, nothing changes.

Maps and directions with no signal

Road and topo maps for all 50 states and DC. Driving and walking directions work offline, even across state lines. Your meeting points are on the map.

Answers from trusted guides

Search guides from FEMA, the CDC, the EPA, the USDA and US Army field manuals. The offline assistant answers from those guides and shows where each answer came from. It runs on your phone, so your questions go nowhere.

Your family's plan, encrypted

Meeting points, contacts, household members and medicines, encrypted on your phone. Share the plan with a nearby phone over Bluetooth or Wi-Fi, with no internet.

Supplies you can count on

Checklists for a go-bag, a car kit and sheltering in place, sized for your household. Scan a barcode to add an item. Get a reminder before food and medicine expire.

No account. No ads. No tracking.

Holdfast doesn't collect your data. Your plan, contacts and supplies stay on your phone, and the private parts are encrypted. We can't see them.

The app goes online only to check for content updates and to download the maps and guides you pick. Read the privacy policy

Where the information comes from

Holdfast is not a government app. No government agency made it or endorses it.
