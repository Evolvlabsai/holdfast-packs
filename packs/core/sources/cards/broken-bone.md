## Neck or back hurt?

Do not move them at all unless they are in danger where they are. Call 911.

## Call 911 if

Bone shows through the skin, there is heavy bleeding, they do not respond, or the hip, thigh or pelvis may be broken.

## Stop any bleeding

Press on the wound with a clean cloth. See the Stop bleeding card.

## Keep it still

Support it the way you found it. Do not push a bone back in or straighten the limb.

## Splint it

Pad a stiff object, such as a board or rolled newspaper, and tie it along the injury so the joints above and below cannot move. If fingers or toes turn pale, cold or numb, loosen it.

## Cold and calm

Put a wrapped cold pack on it. If they turn pale, faint or breathe fast, lay them down, keep them warm, and raise the legs if the legs, hips and back are not hurt.
