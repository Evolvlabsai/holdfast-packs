## Get to high ground

If you are told to leave, leave. If water is rising, move to higher ground now.

## Do not walk in moving water

Six inches of fast water can knock you down.

## Do not drive into water

One foot of moving water can float a car. Two feet can sweep away an SUV or truck. Turn around.

## Car in fast water?

Stay inside. If water rises inside the car, get out onto the roof.

## Trapped in a building?

Go to the highest floor. Do not climb into a closed attic. Go on the roof only if you must, then signal for help.

## After the water

Stay out of floodwater. It can hide sharp objects, sewage and live power lines.
