## Know the signs

Very hot body, confusion, slurred speech, fainting or seizures. The skin may be hot and dry, or soaked with sweat.

## Call 911

Heat stroke is an emergency. Do not wait to see if it gets better.

## Move them to shade

Get them out of the sun into a cool place. Take off extra clothing.

## Cool them fast

Put them in cold water if you can. If not, soak their skin and clothes with water, fan them, and put ice or cold wet cloths on the neck, armpits and groin.

## No drinks if confused

Give nothing to drink if they are confused or not fully awake.

## Only weak, dizzy and sweaty?

That may be heat exhaustion. Move them somewhere cool, loosen clothing, use cool wet cloths and give sips of water. Get help if they throw up, get worse, or are not better in an hour.
