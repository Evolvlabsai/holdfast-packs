## Stop the burning

Get away from the heat. If clothing is on fire: stop, drop and roll.

## Cool it

Hold the burn under cool running water for at least 10 minutes. Do not use ice, butter or ointment.

## Take off rings and tight things

Remove jewelry and tight clothing near the burn before it swells. Leave anything stuck to the skin.

## Cover it loosely

Use a clean, dry, non-stick cloth or sterile gauze. Do not break blisters.

## Call 911 for a bad burn

Large burns; burns on the face, hands, feet or groin; deep burns that look white, brown or charred; chemical or electrical burns; or trouble breathing after a fire.
