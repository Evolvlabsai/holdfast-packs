## Press hard

Put cloth or gauze on the wound and push down firmly with both hands. Do not lift to check.

## Arm or leg still bleeding?

Apply a tourniquet 2–3 inches above the wound, not on a joint. Tighten until bleeding stops.

## Note the time

Write the time the tourniquet went on. Do not loosen it.

## Wound on body or neck?

Pack it tightly with gauze or clean cloth, then keep pressing until help arrives.
