## Face

Ask them to smile. Does one side of the face droop?

## Arms

Ask them to raise both arms. Does one arm drift down?

## Speech

Ask them to repeat a short sentence. Is the speech slurred or strange?

## Balance and eyes

Sudden loss of balance, or sudden trouble seeing in one or both eyes, are signs too.

## Time: call 911

Any one of these signs: call 911 now. Write down the time the signs started. Doctors need it.

## While you wait

Keep them still and comfortable. Give nothing to eat or drink. Do not let them drive or sleep it off.
