## Ask: are you choking?

If they can cough hard or speak, let them keep coughing. Stay with them.

## Give 5 back blows

Stand behind them and lean them forward. Hit firmly between the shoulder blades with the heel of your hand, 5 times.

## Give 5 belly thrusts

Make a fist just above the belly button. Grab it with your other hand. Pull in and up, hard and fast, 5 times.

## Keep going

Switch between 5 back blows and 5 belly thrusts until the object comes out or they can breathe, cough or speak.

## Pregnant or very large?

Put your fist on the center of the chest instead of the belly, and pull straight back.

## If they pass out

Lower them to the floor, call 911 and start CPR. Look in the mouth and take out any object you can see.
