## Text, do not call

Send a short text. Voice calls tie up the networks that emergency crews need.

## Save your battery

Turn on low-power mode, dim the screen and close everything you are not using.

## Tell your out-of-area contact

Message the contact in your plan and let them pass the word on to everyone else.

## Make yourself seen and heard

Three of anything means distress: three whistle blasts, three flashes, three fires. Use a mirror or bright cloth by day.

## Stay where you are if you can

Rescuers search last known locations. If you must move, leave a note saying where you are going and when you left.
