## Know the signs

Adults: shivering, slurred speech, clumsy hands, confusion, drowsiness. Babies: bright red, cold skin and very low energy.

## Get out of the cold

Move the person into a warm room or shelter. Handle them gently and keep them lying still.

## Take off wet clothes

Remove anything wet and dry the skin.

## Warm the middle first

Cover the chest, neck, head and groin with blankets, or use skin-to-skin contact under a blanket. Do not rub the arms and legs.

## Warm drinks only if fully awake

Give warm, sweet, non-alcoholic drinks. Never give alcohol, and never give anything to someone who cannot swallow.

## Keep them covered

Once they start to warm, wrap the whole body including the head and keep them still until help arrives.
