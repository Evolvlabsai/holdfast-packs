## Clear it first

Let cloudy water settle, then pour it through a clean cloth, paper towel or coffee filter.

## Boil if you can

Bring it to a rolling boil for 1 minute. Above 6,500 feet, boil for 3 minutes. Let it cool on its own.

## No heat? Use plain bleach

Per gallon of clear water, add 8 drops of unscented 6% household bleach, or 6 drops of 8.25%. Stir well.

## Wait 30 minutes

The water should smell faintly of bleach. If it does not, repeat the dose and wait another 15 minutes.

## Store it covered

Keep treated water in clean, covered containers out of sunlight.
