## Know the signs

Trouble breathing, wheezing, swelling of the lips, tongue or throat, hives, throwing up, or feeling faint.

## Use their epinephrine

If they have an auto-injector (such as an EpiPen), help them use it right away. Press it into the outer thigh, through clothing if needed, as the label shows.

## Call 911

Call even if they feel better. The reaction can come back.

## Lay them down

If they feel faint, lay them flat and raise their legs. If breathing is hard, let them sit up. Do not let them stand or walk.

## Not better?

If there is no improvement after 5 to 15 minutes and they have a second auto-injector, use it.

## If they stop breathing

Start CPR.
