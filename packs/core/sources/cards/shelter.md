## Get everyone inside

Bring people and pets in. Lock the doors and close the windows, vents and fireplace damper.

## Pick an interior room

Choose a small room with as few windows as possible, and take your kit and radio with you.

## Turn off air movement

Shut off fans, heating and air conditioning so outside air is not pulled in.

## Seal the room only if told to

If officials report a chemical or radiological release, tape plastic sheeting over the doors, windows and vents of that room.

## Listen for the all-clear

Keep a battery or hand-crank radio on NOAA Weather Radio and wait for officials to say it is safe.

## Never burn fuel indoors

No generator, grill or camp stove inside, in a garage or near a window. Carbon monoxide has no smell and kills.
