## Time it

Look at a clock when the seizure starts.

## Make space

Ease them to the floor. Move hard or sharp things away. Put something soft under the head. Loosen anything around the neck.

## Turn them on their side

This helps them breathe. Do not hold them down. Put nothing in their mouth.

## Call 911 if

It lasts more than 5 minutes, another seizure follows, they have trouble breathing or do not wake up, they are hurt, it happened in water, or it is their first seizure.

## Stay with them

Stay until they are fully awake. Talk calmly. They may be confused for a while.
