## Not awake or not breathing?

Call 911 right away. Start CPR if they are not breathing.

## Call Poison Help

Call 1-800-222-1222. Have the container with you. Experts tell you what to do.

## Do not make them throw up

Only do it if Poison Help tells you to.

## On the skin

Take off any clothing it touched. Rinse the skin with running water for 15 to 20 minutes.

## In the eye

Rinse the eye with lukewarm running water for 15 to 20 minutes.

## Fumes

Get to fresh air right away. Carbon monoxide has no smell: leave the building if a CO alarm sounds or several people feel sick, dizzy or sleepy.
