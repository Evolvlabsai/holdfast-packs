## Check and call

Tap the shoulder and shout. If there is no response and no normal breathing, call 911 and put the phone on speaker.

## Hands on the chest

Place the heel of one hand in the center of the chest, your other hand on top, fingers laced. Keep your arms straight.

## Push hard and fast

Push straight down at least 2 inches, 100 to 120 times a minute. Let the chest come all the way back up between pushes.

## Do not stop

Keep going until the person moves and breathes, or until someone takes over. Swap with another person every two minutes if you can.

## Use an AED when it arrives

Turn it on and follow the spoken instructions. Restart compressions as soon as it tells you to.
