## Stop

As soon as you think you are lost, stop moving. Sit down, drink some water and calm down.

## Think

How did you get here? What do you remember passing? Do not walk on unless you are sure.

## Observe

Check the Map tab (it works with no signal), the sun and the land around you.

## Plan: stay put

If you are not sure of the way, stay where you are. Searchers start where you were last seen.

## Be seen and heard

Three of anything means help: three whistle blasts, three shouts, three flashes of a light. Spread bright colors in the open.

## Stay warm and dry

Get out of wind and rain, and off the cold ground. Text someone your location if you get any signal.
