# What goes in a first aid kit

Source: Ready.gov — Build a Kit (FEMA, public domain), with American Red Cross first aid kit guidance

Keep one kit at home and a smaller one in the car. Check both twice a year and replace anything used
or out of date.

## Dressings and bandages

Adhesive bandages in several sizes, sterile gauze pads, a roller bandage, adhesive cloth tape,
triangular bandages and non-stick pads. Add a trauma dressing and a tourniquet if someone in the
household has had the training to use them.

## Tools

Scissors, tweezers, safety pins, disposable non-latex gloves, a breathing barrier for rescue
breaths, an instant cold compress, an oral thermometer and a space blanket.

## Medicines

Pain relievers, an antihistamine, antibiotic ointment, antiseptic wipes, hydrocortisone cream and
oral rehydration salts. Keep a seven-day supply of every prescription the household depends on, plus
a written list of medicines, doses, allergies and prescribing doctors.

## Paperwork

A first aid instruction booklet, emergency phone numbers written on paper, and copies of the
household medical information. Paper still works when the phone is dead.

This is general information, not medical advice. Call 911 whenever you can.
