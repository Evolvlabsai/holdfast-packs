## Pick one small room

Choose a small room near the middle of the house, with few windows. Everyone stays in that room.

## Seal it

![A blanket hung over a window and a rolled towel along the bottom of a door.](seal.svg)

Close the door and put a rolled towel along the bottom. Hang blankets over the windows at night. Open the curtains when the sun shines in.

## Make a blanket tent

![Blankets draped over a table, with two people inside.](tent.svg)

Drape blankets over a table or bunk and get inside together. Shared body heat keeps a small space warm.

## Dress and eat for warmth

Wear a hat, dry socks and loose layers. Eat regular meals and drink water. Skip alcohol: it makes you lose heat.

## No grills or stoves inside

![A grill inside a house, crossed out.](no-grill.svg)

Never bring a grill, camp stove or generator inside, even with a window open. Keep the CO alarm on.
