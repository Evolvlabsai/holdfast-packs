title: Shortening candle
summary: A tub of vegetable shortening and a cotton wick make a candle that burns for many hours.
category: light
difficulty: easy
time: 10 minutes
order: 20
needs:
  - A tub or can of solid vegetable shortening (like Crisco)
  - A cotton candle wick, or 100% cotton string
  - A pencil, chopstick or skewer
  - A heat-proof plate
  - Matches or a lighter
warnings:
  - Never leave a burning candle alone, and blow it out before you sleep.
  - Keep it away from curtains, paper, children and pets.
  - If the grease catches fire, cover it with a metal lid. Never pour water on a grease fire.
  - Use flashlights when you can. Candles start many fires during power outages.

## Open the tub

![An open tub of shortening with its lid lifted off.](open.svg)

Take the lid off. Use a tub that is metal or very thick; thin plastic can melt.

## Push the wick down the middle

![A wick going straight down the center of the shortening to the bottom.](wick.svg)

Poke a hole straight down the center with the pencil. Push the wick into it all the way to the bottom.

## Leave a short wick on top

Leave about half an inch of wick standing up. Rub a little shortening on it so it lights easily.

## Light it on a plate

![The lit candle sitting on a wide plate.](lit.svg)

Set the tub on a heat-proof plate on a steady table. Light the wick. Do not move it while it burns.
